AUTO-FLAG FIX (user request 2026-11-18; commits 320e480 and 0f61681)
- Request: "Fix the autoflag of the system. It auto flags a kiosk scan in the scan logs and
  quality control even though the average confidence of the kiosk scan is more than the
  default of 80% confidence threshold."
- Decision: "The auto flag must work in confidence is below the threshold only. No other
  factors needed." Old flags: "Clean up with a preview".
- backend/app/services/fusion_engine.py: auto_flagged = final confidence < review
  threshold, nothing else. FusedResult.flag_reason holds the sentence. The other signals
  (cultivar/ripeness/view conflicts, weak pair support, insufficient views, missing
  captures, different finger counts) are FusedResult.observations.
- backend/app/services/scan_service.py: flags only on fusion's rule. Dropped boxes and
  observations go to the scan_created activity entry after "; noted: ...". A flagged scan's
  created history entry, activity entry and notification say "Confidence X% is below the
  Y% threshold".
- backend/scripts/evaluate_finger_count.py mirrors the rule and reports "noted".
- backend/scripts/review_auto_flags.py: station command for old flags. Preview by default.
  `--apply --admin <username>` removes flags the rule would not set. It never touches
  scans below the threshold, scans a person flagged/corrected/noted, or scans without a
  stored confidence. NOT run by the assistant; the station admin runs it.
- Frontend: the Scan Detail cultivar-disagreement notice mentions clearing the flag only
  when the scan is flagged.
- Docs: Development Decisions (2026-11-18 entry), Resolved Bugs, AI Models, Testing and
  Validation, backend README.
