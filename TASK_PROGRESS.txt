ROOT CAUSE (code reading + measurement)
- Stage 2 (12-class cultivar YOLO) is closed-set and was trained without background
  images: any box it emits is a supported cultivar.
- The COCO banana pre-detector passed a scan when any one camera saw a banana, then
  Stage 2 ran on all views and every box voted and counted.
- The pre-detector failed open when it could not run.
- Measured single-frame leak of the earlier gate on the held-out benchmark: near-OOD
  3.28 % (22/670), far-OOD 0.14 % (1/708). Four unrelated views compound this to 6.55 %.
- Stage 2's own false-cultivar rate, measured on the laptop on 2026-11-13 (details under
  LAPTOP EVALUATION): with no gate it names a cultivar on 76.42 % of near-OOD and
  61.24 % of far-OOD photos.

DONE
- backend/app/services/ood_gate.py: pure gate logic, policy, rejection codes.
- banana_detector.py: banana evidence boxes at 0.05, `available` flag.
- classification.py: a cultivar box whose centre is not covered by banana evidence
  (grown 25 %) is treated as if the model never emitted it. It is skipped in the first
  pass, the rotated Top pass and the occluded-finger recovery pass before any other rule
  sees it; the team's rules (V2 checkpoints, canonical rotation, TTA recovery, cluster
  and duplicate rules, genuine max-view counting) are not modified, and nothing changes
  when no evidence is given. Every scan the gate altered is flagged: in the first and
  recovery passes a skipped box counts unless that pass would have dropped it anyway;
  in the rotated Top pass every skipped box counts, because a repeat there still steers
  the orientation and label-conflict logic. A Top scan can so be flagged for a skipped
  box that changed nothing; the reverse (a silent change) does not happen.
- scan_service.py / api/v1/scans.py: gate on every view; rejection 422 with detail +
  code/status/is_ood/reason; same fields on scan:failed; any scan that lost a box is
  saved flagged; scan_rejected activity row with per-view banana scores. Faults answer
  503 with is_ood false: verifier unavailable (fail closed) and no camera frame.
- config.py / .env.example: OOD_BANANA_CONFIDENCE 0.30, OOD_SUPPORT_CONFIDENCE 0.05,
  OOD_SUPPORT_MARGIN 0.25, OOD_BOX_SUPPORT_FILTER true, OOD_MIN_BANANA_VIEWS 1,
  OOD_GATE_FAIL_OPEN false. No database migration.
- backend/scripts/prepare_ood_dataset.py + scripts/ood/open_images_ood_manifest.json:
  Open Images V7 pools (train_negatives 998, calibration 1284, benchmark 1654), disjoint
  by image ID and file hash; benchmark negatives also come from a different official
  split. merge-negatives copies a prepared YOLO dataset and adds background images to
  train only. backend/ood-data/ is git-ignored.
- backend/scripts/evaluate_ood_gate.py: `gate` mode (run here) and `pipeline` mode
  (needs trained weights + labeled split). Pipeline mode groups the prepared files of
  one capture into a scan and scores it as the station does (scan gate over the views,
  then each view with its own camera label and banana evidence), reports each view, and
  says under `top_paths` whether the Top refinement, rotated pass and recovery ran.
  Fusion across views is not simulated.
- Frontend: api.ts error code/reason; ScanContext refusal state with one notice per
  scan; Kiosk status card "No Lakatan, Latundan, or Cardaba detected" and an alert card
  "Scan could not run" for 503 faults; Activity Log badge and full detail; Profile label.
- Docs: backend/README.md, docs/knowledge/AI Models.md (new), Frontend.md, Testing and
  Validation.md, Development Decisions.md, the gate passages of the presentation guide
  (docs/kent_presentation_guide 00 and 08), docs/audits/ood-gate-evaluation-2026-11-13.json.

VERIFICATION
- Held-out benchmark (gate only): near-OOD frame rejection 96.72 % -> 97.31 %, far-OOD
  99.86 % -> 100 %, generic banana frames accepted 90.07 %, AUROC 0.976, AUPR 0.936,
  FPR at 95 % TPR 2.83 %. Box support keeps 94.92 % of banana boxes; 7.3 % of near-OOD
  and 0.16 % of far-OOD object boxes pass. One banana view plus three unrelated views:
  OOD boxes admitted 11069 -> 475.
- Backend: 685 passed, 104 skipped (PostgreSQL-only; no disposable cluster here);
  the team main (0c74c73) alone gives 582 passed, 104 skipped. 103 new tests.
- Frontend: 280 browser tests, 5 new kiosk cases. Full run 279 passed, 1 failed
  (397.1 s): kiosk-pin "PIN verification sends once..." stopped in its own setup with
  "clock.pauseAt: Cannot fast-forward to the past". That test, its fixture and the
  harness are unchanged, and the file passed 15/15 on three reruns, so it is a
  Node-versus-browser clock race, not a regression. Typecheck and build passed; lint
  40 problems (35 errors, 5 warnings), unchanged baseline.
- Ruff: new files clean; changed backend files 43 findings, the same as at 0c74c73.
- Reviews: a design review, then four separate reviews of the finished change
  (correctness, security, API/UI contract, tests and documentation) raised 20 concrete
  points. All were addressed: suppression by unsupported boxes, votes of removed boxes,
  camera fault shown as a rejection, incomplete downloads, link write-through, data.yaml
  relocation, line cap, unignored example paths, notice merging, fault display, contrast,
  hidden audit detail, and six tests that could not fail for the defect they named.
  Seven tests were confirmed by mutating the code they guard, including each of the
  three places where the classifier applies the gate.
- A further review after merging 0c74c73 raised 8 points, all addressed: a tight-evidence
  case that emptied a real banana view, a set-aside box changing the cluster size check,
  scans flagged although the gate changed nothing (these three led to the simpler
  "never emitted" design), the laptop evaluation not passing the camera view, the wrong
  weights file in the command, and an inaccurate "disjoint by split" statement.
- A randomized confirmation of about 146,000 cases found the gate-off path identical to
  0c74c73 and the gate result identical to the classifier on the supported boxes, but
  2,044 Top cases where a skipped rotated-pass box changed the result uncounted. Fixed
  by counting every skipped rotated-pass box; rerun of about 61,000 cases: 0 violations.
  It also led to scan-level scoring, the `top_paths` status and an early output check in
  the evaluation script.
- No live station database, captures, cameras, sensors or OS power were used.

LIMITS / KNOWLEDGE
- NOT measured: any result on a registered Top-calibrated checkpoint (Top refinement,
  rotated pass, finger recovery), how many real scans the gate would flag for review,
  and four-camera fusion. No retraining with negatives was run.
- Unsupported banana cultivars pass the gate (COCO "banana" covers all varieties).
- Strong lookalikes pass the verifier (yellow watermelon 0.90, mango slice 0.89, yellow
  squash 0.84); retraining Stage 2 with the hard negatives is the remedy.
- Green or peeled bananas score low (0.09-0.21); unripe station hands are unmeasured.
  If valid scans are flagged or lose fingers, set OOD_BOX_SUPPORT_FILTER=false and report.
- Thresholds come from open-source photos, not station captures.
- Graphify CLI/graph, ICM and Context7 were unavailable; graph and memory not refreshed.
  No Codex Security service scan was run; source review only.

Top-calibrated file, so `top_paths.active` was false: the Top refinement, rotated pass
and finger recovery did not run. The report files are in docs/audits/ with their
content unchanged (line endings converted to LF).

Run 2 - real bananas (docs/audits/ood-pipeline-labeled-split-2026-11-13.json)
- Weights: best.pt of training run v26s_run2_zick, SHA-256 starting fe9359d5.
- Data: test split of the Roboflow export Banana_Ripeness.v17i.yolo26; 416 photos with
  one labeled cultivar (Cardaba 134, Lakatan 155, Latundan 127), grouped into 337 scans.
- Photos that end with a cultivar, and finger boxes:
    no gate       416/416 (100 %)     3,534 boxes
    earlier gate  409/416 (98.32 %)   3,467 boxes
    OOD gate      408/416 (98.08 %)   3,453 boxes
  No photo received a wrong cultivar in any variant (precision 1.0 throughout).
- Scan gate: 330 of 337 scans accepted. The 7 rejected scans hold one photo each and
  are rejected by the earlier gate as well (same verifier rule).
- OOD gate against the earlier gate: 1 more photo without a cultivar (a Cardaba) and
  14 fewer finger boxes (0.40 %), all in the Rear view. The totals for Top, Left and
  Right are identical in both.
- Verifier below acceptance (0.30): Top 0/99, Left 2/94, Right 2/128, Rear 6/95. The
  3 such frames that sit inside an accepted scan are all Rear frames.
- Limits of run 2: the test photos may overlap this checkpoint's training data, so the
  perfect no-gate score may read high. The scan-gate decisions use only the COCO
  verifier and are unaffected by that; the box-level differences (photos without a
  cultivar, finger boxes) are counted on this checkpoint's boxes for these photos and
  may differ on unseen photos. Most scans are a single photo and fusion is not
  simulated. The report does not count flagged scans (at least one accepted scan lost
  boxes: one Rear photo lost all of them). If that photo were the only view of its
  scan, the station would reject the scan instead of flagging it; the report cannot
  tell. The finger-box figures are totals, so they show the net change only.
- Against the criteria set before the run: NOT met. They asked that recall and
  `finger_boxes` do not drop and that `scans_accepted` equals `scans`. Measured: 330
  of 337 scans accepted (the earlier gate rejects the same 7), one more photo without
  a cultivar, 14 fewer finger boxes.
- Decision: no code or threshold change, because the extra cost is small, limited to
  the Rear totals, and thresholds are not tuned on a test split. Whether that cost is
  acceptable on the station is the team's call.
