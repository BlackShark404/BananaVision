ROOT CAUSE
- Web QR codes open /track/<scan id> (frontend/src/lib/qrcode.ts). PublicScanView had a
  header "Log in" button, a "Need more details?" login card, a "Log in" button on the
  error and not-found screens, and redirected signed-in viewers to the staff page.
- Found in review: App.tsx mounted /track inside AdminProvider, ScanProvider and
  InventoryProvider. With a stored staff session those providers loaded staff data and
  opened the staff socket on the QR page; an expired or revoked token then hit the 401
  handler (lib/api.ts) or the 4001 socket close (useWebSocket.ts) and sent the phone to
  /login.
- The public track response listed internal capture paths and camera IDs.
- Thermal printer labels hold a plain-text QR payload; not affected.
