DONE BY THIS SESSION (2026-11-14)
- Finger-counting post-processing and two-tier multi-camera fusion fixes in
  backend/app/services/classification.py, backend/app/services/fusion_engine.py, and
  frontend/src/lib/detectionBoxes.ts:
  1. _is_axially_aligned_or_enclosed checks orientation-aware longitudinal alignment and
     preserves stacked horizontal flank fingers and narrow protruding second-tier fingers
     while keeping whole-hand box splits and small fragment suppression.
  2. _is_cluster_box requires containment >= 0.82 and matching child orientation
     ((a.w > a.h) == (b.w > b.h)) so 45-degree diagonal fingers in radial fans are not
     dropped when their bounding-box corners overlap a horizontal and a vertical neighbor.
  3. _is_duplicate_detection treats sideways/diagonal pairs (w > h on both boxes) as
     horizontal at cont >= 0.45, preserves parallel horizontal/vertical fingers separated
     by > 0.30 * min_dim at 0.40 < IoU < 0.65 in the rotated Top pass, and
     _is_recovery_duplicate suppresses TTA strip repeats.
  4. _ripeness_outliers preserves connected groups of >= 2 unique far boxes touching the
     hand so adjacent stage-straddling fingers are kept; _hides_fingers includes same-cultivar
     cross-stage sub-boxes when splitting whole-hand boxes.
  5. yolo_peripheral_background_filter preserves slender upper fingers clipped by the
     left/right frame edge (x1 <= 0.04 * img_w or x2 >= 0.96 * img_w) or overlapping the
     accepted hand.
  6. Pass 3 recovery runs when cultivar or ripeness consensus >= 60% holds, admitting
     candidates whose (cultivar, ripeness) label was already observed in Pass 1/2 or
     adjacent-stage same-cultivar candidates on multi-stage hands.
  7. FusionEngine._estimate_multi_camera_fingers reconstructs occluded lower-tier fingers on
     verified two-tier 4-camera hands using 2D vertical tier separation
     (_split_horizontal_tiers and _count_top_second_tier) while keeping single-tier hands at
     max(finger_counts).
