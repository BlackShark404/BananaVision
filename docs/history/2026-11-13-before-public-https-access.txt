ROOT CAUSE (code reading + tests)
- The stored value is the string "1-3" in scan_classification.spoilage_days; the word
  "days" is added only for display. Nothing recomputed it, by design.
- Kiosk recent scans and Quality Control showed only that stored estimate. Scan Logs
  showed it as the main cell text.
- The existing countdown was one number: the range midpoint minus elapsed seconds. It
  was not a range and did not follow calendar days, so "1-3" read "Overdue" after two
  days and nothing ever showed "0-2 days".

RULE (one implementation: backend/app/services/inventory_policy.py)
- elapsed_days = ManilaDate(now) - ManilaDate(scan.date_time), never below zero. Dates
  come from the existing as_manila helper (backend/app/core/time.py).
- Whole-day bounds of the stored estimate: a range keeps its ends; a decimal spans
  floor to ceiling; zero stays zero; a positive value below one day counts as one day.
  This is the rule the estimate is displayed with (frontend/src/lib/spoilageDisplay.ts).
- remaining_min = max(0, min - elapsed_days); remaining_max = max(0, max - elapsed_days).
- state: "final_day" when elapsed_days equals the upper bound, "expired" when it is
  greater, otherwise "active". days_past = max(0, elapsed_days - max).
- Nothing is stored or rewritten. No migration. Unreadable estimates and missing
  timestamps give no window and never raise.

LIMITS / KNOWLEDGE
- The at-risk icon, counts and Inventory labels use the midpoint number; the countdown on
  the other pages counts calendar days over the whole range. They can differ near the
  ends of a range (examples in docs/knowledge/Frontend.md).

  number always did.
